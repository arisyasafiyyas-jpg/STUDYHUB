Study Techniques 📚

Discover effective learning methods to improve your understanding and memory.

Pomodoro Technique

Study for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break. Repeat the cycle to stay focused and avoid burnout.

How to Use

  • 25 minutes study
  • 5 minutes break
  • Repeat 4 sessions
  • Take a longer break

Active Recall

Test yourself instead of rereading your notes. This strengthens long-term memory.

Example

  • Close your notes
  • Answer questions from memory
  • Check your answers

Feynman Technique

Explain a topic in simple language as if teaching someone else.

Steps

  • Choose a topic
  • Explain simply
  • Find missing knowledge
  • Review again

Spaced Repetition

Review information over increasing intervals to improve memory retention.

Review Plan

  • Day 1
  • Day 3
  • Day 7
  • Day 14

Mind Mapping

Connect ideas visually using diagrams and branches.

Best For

  • History
  • Biology
  • Business

Blurting

Write down everything you remember before checking your notes.

Steps

  • Read once
  • Close the book
  • Write from memory
